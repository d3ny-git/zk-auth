Zk-Auth

Prove you are over 18 and hold an issuer's credential, without revealing your date of birth, your credential, or which credential it is. Every step runs in this tab; watch each one light up.

  1. 1 Load credential

    Your cred.json (secret + date of birth) and path.json (Merkle path) from the issuer. They are kept in memory only, never stored and never sent.

    …or paste them

  2. 2 Connect wallet

    Your Ethereum address becomes your account on this site. The wallet will later sign one message; it never sees your credential.

  3. 3 Get a nonce

    The server hands out a one-time number (valid 120 s), today's age cutoff and its site ID. All three go into the proof, so it cannot be replayed elsewhere or later.

  4. 4 Prove (in a Web Worker)

    libsnark, compiled to WebAssembly, builds a Groth16 proof of "my credential is in the issuer's tree, I was born on or before the cutoff". The proving key (~150 MB) is downloaded once and cached.

  5. 5 Sign in with Ethereum

    An EIP-4361 message whose statement carries the proof's hash, signed with personal_sign. This ties the proof to your wallet.

  6. 6 Log in

    POST /login runs the server's check chain and returns a session token, which is then used to call GET /me.

  7. 7 What the server saw

    The exact request body sent to /login.

Attack lab

Each button sends a deliberately broken login. Every one must be rejected; the badge shows which server check caught it (debug header, development mode only).
